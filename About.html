About ProForma Suite

Small apps,
built to actually help.

We build focused, single-purpose productivity software — the kind that does one job well instead of everything badly. No bloat, no dark patterns, no pretending your documents need to touch our servers to work.

Our Mission

Fewer clicks. Fewer subscriptions you forget about. More work actually done.

Most productivity software tries to be everything at once — a suite of half-finished features bolted together. We do the opposite: each ProForma Suite app solves exactly one real, everyday problem, and solves it well. DOCX Optimizer cleans up documents. ProForm Form Manager builds and fills forms. ProRedactor finds and removes sensitive information. Synthetix-CR bundles the two you'll use together most, at a lower combined price. That's it — no feature creep, no upsells buried in menus.

What We Believe

Three things we won't compromise on.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Your documents, forms, and files are processed locally on your device. The only thing that ever reaches our servers is a device fingerprint and timestamp for license verification — never your content.

🎯

One job, done properly

Every app in the suite is built around a single task. We'd rather ship a small tool that never gets in your way than a bloated one that tries to do everything.

🤝

Honest, simple pricing

One monthly price per app, a real 30-day free trial with no credit card required to start, and cancel anytime — no long-term contracts, no surprise renewals, no fine print designed to trap you.
