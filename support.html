Free Trial
The trial gives you full Premium access to the app for 90 calendar days from the date you register — no credit card required, no obligation. Here's the full process:
  1. Click Start Free Trial on any app page and enter your email address to register.
  2. You receive a trial activation link by email. Click it to confirm your address and start the clock.
  3. Download the app from the Download Portal using your trial credentials.
  4. Install and use the full Premium version for 90 days — every feature, no nags, no limits.
  5. On day 90, the app automatically transitions to the free Lite version. No action required from you, and no charge is ever made without a separate, explicit purchase on your part.
See our Terms of Use and EULA for full details.
When the 90-day trial expires, the app automatically reverts to the Lite version — a permanent, free tier with no expiration date. Specifically:
  • No data loss. Every file, document, and project you created during the trial is fully preserved and remains accessible.
  • No action required. The transition happens automatically. You don't need to uninstall or reinstall anything.
  • No charge. The reversion to Lite is silent and free. You will never be billed without completing an explicit purchase.
  • Premium features are disabled in the Lite version. Core functionality remains available.
You can upgrade to Premium at any time by purchasing a license — your data and settings carry over instantly.
The Lite version is a permanent, free tier of each ProForma Suite app — yours to keep indefinitely with no expiration, no subscription, and no cost. It includes the core functionality of the app; advanced Premium features are disabled but can be re-enabled at any time with a one-time license purchase. The specific features available in each app's Lite version are detailed on each product page.
Purchase & License Keys
Your license key is delivered automatically by email as soon as your payment is confirmed — typically within a few minutes. Here's what to expect:
  1. Complete your purchase on the secure payment page (Stripe or Gumroad). You'll see a confirmation screen immediately.
  2. An email is sent to the address you used at checkout. It contains your unique license key — a code in the format XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX — and a link to the Download Portal.
  3. If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam or junk folder. Payment processors sometimes trigger spam filters.
  4. Still nothing? Use the contact form with your order ID and we'll resend your key within 24 hours.
Your key is tied to your purchase record. Keep the email — it's your permanent proof of license.
When you enter your key in the Download Portal and activate it within the app, the following happens:
  • The app generates a unique Hardware ID from your machine's hardware — this happens entirely on your device; nothing sensitive is transmitted.
  • Your license key is cryptographically bound to that Hardware ID. It will only work on this specific machine going forward.
  • All Premium features unlock immediately — no restart required.
  • After activation, the app works fully offline. No internet connection is needed to use any feature.
This is a one-machine license. Attempting to activate the same key on a second device will fail. See our EULA §3 for the full machine-binding policy.
