ProForm

Create professional forms from scratch, or generate them automatically from existing documents.

ProForm converts plain text or Microsoft Word files into fully interactive forms through a guided, point-and-click workflow, with drag-and-drop reordering for fine-tuning field order.

Paragraph structure is detected automatically and mapped to fields and their dropdown options, significantly reducing the manual effort of building forms from scratch.

Add subforms and conditional logic that reveal or hide fields based on a user's answers, keeping complex workflows organized and data accurate.

Export the finished form as a polished Word or PDF document, or as plain text, ready to print, share, or insert directly into your reporting workflow.

Keep your work safe
Forms you build are saved in this browser by default, but browser data can be cleared. Link a folder on your computer now so every form — and your progress — is also saved as a real file, so nothing is ever lost.
Recommended: Create a folder called My Forms in your Documents, then click Choose a folder below and select it. You can link or unlink a folder any time from the sidebar.

Set up your backup folder
Forms you build, and forms you fill in, are saved in this browser by default — but browser data can be cleared or lost. Link a real folder on your computer now so every form and every filled session is also kept there as a permanent file.
Recommended: In the folder picker, open Documents, click New folder, name it My Forms, then select it. Every custom form and every filled session saves there automatically from then on — no separate folder needed for filled forms.

    Preview & clean up
    Step 1: click a line below to select it (drag, or shift-click, to select several lines at once). Step 2: click one of the buttons below to tell ProForm what that selected text should become in your finished form — for example, marking it as the form's Headline, a Dropdown block of choices, or an Info box note (always shown in italics). When you select several lines and tag them as a Dropdown block, they're combined into one field — the first selected line becomes the field name, and the rest become its options, even if they originally came from different paragraphs. Anything left unmarked and not deleted is treated as a normal dropdown block automatically. To import a whole section as its own Subform, select its heading line plus the field paragraphs under it (each still first line = field name, rest = options) and click Subform — you'll name it and choose which answer reveals it on the next screen. Double-click any line to fix a typo or edit its text directly.
