ProForm — Form Manager
Professional forms.
Zero dependencies.

A purpose-built form management tool that converts structured data into intelligent, interactive forms and produces polished Word documents — with no installation, no internet dependency, and complete data privacy.

What ProForm Does

ProForm replaces slow, generic form tools with a system designed around one goal: turning structured information into a professional Word document as efficiently as possible. It combines smart dropdown-driven forms, conditional logic, drag-and-drop reordering, and multi-format export into a single HTML file you open in any browser — nothing else required.

Key Strengths
Intelligent Forms

Pre-built dropdown lists and multi-select checkboxes eliminate free-text errors and ensure consistent, reportable data across every submission.

Conditional Logic

Forms adapt in real time. Selecting an option can reveal or hide entire sections, keeping the interface clean and relevant to each unique case.

Conditional Subforms

Complex scenarios trigger dedicated sub-sections automatically — users only ever see fields that apply to their specific situation.

Build a New Form

Build or modify forms entirely within the app. Drag-and-drop reordering, required field enforcement, and multi-level conditional rules — no coding required.

Guided Examples

Built-in forms demonstrate dropdowns, conditional subforms, and required fields, and stay locked so they're always available as a reliable reference. Duplicate one to customize freely, or hide it once you've found your footing.

Multi-format Export

Completed forms export to Word (.doc), PDF, or plain text in one click — ready for reporting, filing, or sharing immediately.

Named Sessions

Work can be saved mid-completion and resumed at any time, with multiple named sessions stored simultaneously across different forms.

Multi-select Fields

Fields can be configured as checkboxes for scenarios requiring multiple selections — exported as clean dashed lists in Word, PDF, and text formats.

Info Boxes

Add read-only instructional notes anywhere in a form — visible while filling it out. Info boxes are excluded from every export format (plain text, PDF, Word); exports contain only section headlines and the answers the user selected.

Undo Support

Made a mistake filling in a form? Ctrl+Z steps back up to five recent changes, no confirmation dialogs required.

Library Backup & Restore

Export your entire custom form library to a single JSON file and restore it anytime — on this device or a new one — without losing anything to a cleared browser cache.

My Forms Folder Link

In Chrome or Edge, link a real folder on your computer to save custom forms — and every filled session — as individual files, in addition to browser storage. They reload automatically next time — no separate backup step required. ProForm even offers to link a folder the first time you start building, before any work exists to lose.

Bold & Italic Auto-Detection on Import

When importing a .docx file, ProForm reads bold and italic formatting and automatically pre-tags bold paragraphs as field headers and italic paragraphs as Info box content in the cleanup screen — jumpstarting the review process. Plain text, paste, and .doc imports are unchanged.

My Forms & Filled Forms Libraries

Two library tabs in the top bar give instant access to your form collection. My Forms Library lists every custom form with a completeness badge, sortable by most used, A–Z, or newest first. Filled Forms lists every saved session with a progress percentage — amber for a partial fill, green once complete — showing your 10 most recent by default with a link to see older ones. Click any entry to open it directly, or find either one with the sidebar's search box.

Side-by-Side Import Review

While confirming each field pulled from an imported document, the original source text stays visible alongside the review form — with the relevant lines highlighted — so you can verify every field against the source as you go. Split one block of text into two separate fields on the fly, step back to fix a field you just confirmed, or pause a long import to finish it later without losing your place.

Server Independence & Privacy

Your form data never leaves your device — by design, not by policy

ProForm is a single HTML file with no backend for your forms to talk to. Form logic, data storage, and document generation all run entirely inside your browser — your answers, sessions, and custom forms are never transmitted anywhere, full stop. The one narrow exception, covered in full below, is licence enforcement: a minimal, anonymous check-in used only to verify your trial or activation status, which never carries any form content.

  • No server, no cloud account, no login — open the file and use it immediately.
  • All form data — answers, sessions, custom forms — is stored in your browser's local storage on your own machine by default; nothing about it leaves your device, ever.
  • Building, filling, and exporting forms functions fully offline, on air-gapped machines, behind firewalls, or in any network-restricted environment.
  • No third-party scripts, no CDN calls, no analytics, and no tracking. The only network activity anywhere in the app is a small licence check-in (a device fingerprint and a timestamp — nothing identifying, nothing from your forms) used to keep trial enforcement honest, plus your email address if you choose to purchase a licence. If you're offline, ProForm simply uses the last confirmed status and keeps working.
  • Word, PDF, and text exports are generated and saved locally by your browser — no upload, conversion service, or intermediary server is ever involved.
  • You control your own backups: export a session anytime, or back up your entire custom form library to a portable JSON file.
  • The optional "My Forms" folder link (Chrome/Edge) saves custom forms as individual files in a folder you explicitly choose, in addition to browser storage — still entirely on your device, with nothing outside that folder ever touched or transmitted anywhere.
  • Compatible with all modern browsers: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari.

Trial & Activation

ProForm opens as a single HTML file. When you first open it, a 30-day free trial begins automatically — no payment, no account, no restrictions. All features are fully unlocked during the trial. Build as many forms as you like, delete and redo them freely; nothing is counted.

After the trial, a one-time activation key is required to continue using premium features. To get a key: click Enter licence key in the app top bar → Buy ProForm for instant checkout and email delivery, or Request key by email to arrange payment manually. Keys are unique to your device and, once entered, validate instantly and work fully offline from then on — no repeated internet connection required to keep using an activated copy.
