Getting Started

Step 1 — How do I unzip and set up the downloaded file?

▾

Do not run files directly from inside the compressed ZIP archive. First, extract it:

  • Windows: Right-click the downloaded ZIP file → select Extract All… → choose a destination (Desktop or Documents) → click Extract.
  • macOS: Double-click the ZIP file. macOS extracts it automatically into the same folder.

We recommend naming the extracted folder after the app you downloaded (e.g. DOCX Optimizer) so it is easy to find later.

Step 2 — How do I launch the app?

▾

Open the extracted folder. Inside, locate the file named index.html — it will display your default browser icon (Edge, Chrome, Safari, or Firefox). Double-click index.html to launch the app. It opens directly in your browser and runs entirely on your local machine — no installation required and no internet connection needed after activation.

Step 3 — What happens on first launch?

▾

On your very first launch you will be presented with a Compliance Notice covering third-party copyright acknowledgements and network usage limitations. Read and accept this notice to proceed.

You will then be prompted to enter your License Key — the unique activation code sent to your email address by our billing system after your subscription is confirmed. Enter it exactly as received.

Once activated, the app runs 100% offline for the remainder of your billing cycle. Your data never leaves your machine.

Purchase & License Keys

How is my license key delivered after purchase?

▾

Your license key is delivered automatically by email as soon as your payment is confirmed — typically within a few minutes:

  1. Complete your purchase on the secure payment page. You'll see a confirmation screen immediately.
  2. An email is sent containing your unique license key — a code in the format XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX — and a link to the Download Portal.
  3. If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  4. Still nothing? Contact us with your order ID and we'll resend your key within 24 hours.

Your key is tied to your purchase record. Keep the email — it's your permanent proof of license.

What happens when I enter my license key?

▾

When you enter your key in the Download Portal and activate it within the app:

  • The app generates a unique Hardware ID from your machine — this happens entirely on your device; nothing sensitive is transmitted.
  • Your license key is cryptographically bound to that Hardware ID. It will only work on this specific machine going forward.
  • All Premium features unlock immediately — no restart required.
  • After activation, the app works fully offline. No internet connection is needed to use any feature.

This is a one-machine license. Attempting to activate the same key on a second device will fail. See EULA §3 for the full machine-binding policy.

How do I download my purchased app?

▾

Visit the Download Portal and enter the license key from your purchase email. The Portal validates your key and unlocks the download for your operating system. You'll always receive the latest version — future updates are included and accessed the same way using the same key.

I lost my license key — what do I do?

▾

Check the original confirmation email from your payment processor first — your key and order ID are both in that email. If you can't find it, contact us with your purchase email address and order ID. We'll verify your purchase and resend your key within 24 hours, Monday to Friday. We cannot resend keys without order verification.

Managing Your License

Can I use my license on multiple computers?

▾

No. Each license is bound to one machine at the point of activation — enforced by Hardware ID binding built into the app. A single key cannot be activated on a second device; the attempt will be rejected. Each machine requires its own separately purchased license at the standard price. There are no multi-seat discounts. See EULA §3 and §5 for the full policy.

How do I get app updates?

▾

Updates are available through the Download Portal — enter your license key to always download the latest version. Updates to your licensed major version are included at no extra charge. Your key never expires.

My machine was replaced — can I move my license?

▾

Yes — a one-time license transfer is available if your machine is decommissioned due to hardware failure, loss, or replacement. Contact us with your order ID and a brief description of the situation. Transfers are reviewed and processed within 2–3 business days. Note that significant hardware modifications (such as a motherboard replacement) may also require re-binding.

App & Technical

My app won't open or install.

▾

First, confirm your operating system meets the requirements listed on each app's detail page. On macOS, navigate to System Settings → Privacy & Security and allow the app if prompted. On Windows, click "More info → Run anyway" on the SmartScreen dialog. If the issue persists, contact us with your OS version and app version number.

Does the app require an internet connection?

▾

No. All ProForma Suite apps run entirely on your local machine with no internet connection required after the initial license activation. There are no server-side functions, no cloud processing, and no telemetry. Your documents never leave your device. The only network event is the one-time activation check when you first enter your license key.

Do you offer refunds?

▾

Yes — we offer a 14-day refund from the date of purchase if the software doesn't work as described. Contact us with your order ID and a brief description of the issue. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days via your original payment method, no questions asked.
