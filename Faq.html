Getting Started

Step 1 — How do I unzip and set up the downloaded file?

▾

Do not run files directly from inside the compressed ZIP archive. First, extract it:

  • Windows: Right-click the downloaded ZIP file → select Extract All… → choose a destination (Desktop or Documents) → click Extract.
  • macOS: Double-click the ZIP file. macOS extracts it automatically into the same folder.

We recommend naming the extracted folder after the app you downloaded (e.g. DOCX Optimizer) so it is easy to find later.

Step 2 — How do I launch the app?

▾

Open the extracted folder. Inside, locate the file named index.html — it will display your default browser icon (Edge, Chrome, Safari, or Firefox). Double-click index.html to launch the app. It opens directly in your browser and runs entirely on your local machine — no installation required and no internet connection needed after activation.

Step 3 — What happens on first launch?

▾

Nothing to enter, nothing to unlock — your 30-day free trial starts automatically the moment you open the app. There's no upfront license key, sign-in, or compliance notice blocking you from working.

Already subscribed? Click Enter license key in the app's top bar at any time to activate your Premium subscription — it takes effect immediately.

Subscription & License Keys

How is my license key delivered after I subscribe?

▾

Your license key is delivered automatically by email as soon as your first payment is confirmed — typically within a few minutes:

  1. Subscriptions start from inside the app itself (Enter license key → Subscribe in the top bar), which takes you to a secure Stripe checkout page. You'll see a confirmation screen immediately after your first payment.
  2. An email is sent containing your license key — a long signed code, not a short PIN — for you to paste into the app's activation box.
  3. If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  4. Still nothing? Contact us with your order ID and we'll help you get it resent.

Your key is tied to your subscription. Keep the email — it's your proof of subscription.

What happens when I enter my license key?

▾

When you enter your key inside the app and click activate:

  • The app computes a device fingerprint in your browser — based on things like your screen, timezone, and browser configuration, not your physical hardware serial numbers. This happens entirely on your device.
  • Your license key is a cryptographically signed record tied to that fingerprint. It's built to work on this specific browser/device going forward.
  • All Premium features unlock immediately — no restart required.
  • Once activated, the app keeps working fully offline — no internet connection is needed to use any feature. When you do have a connection, it briefly re-confirms your subscription status in the background, so a canceled subscription or a locally-forged clock can't be used to fake continued access.

This is a one-machine license. Activating the same key on a different browser or device produces a different fingerprint, so it won't validate there. See EULA §3 for the full device-binding policy.

How do I download my subscribed app?

▾

Visit the Download Portal — every available app is downloadable immediately, no key required. Downloading gets you the same file whether you're trying it free or already subscribed; you activate Premium afterward from inside the app itself using the key from your subscription confirmation email. You'll always get the latest version this way, and future updates are included at no extra charge while your subscription is active.

I lost my license key — what do I do?

Managing Your License

Can I use my license on multiple computers?

▾

No. Each license is bound to one device at the point of activation, based on a browser fingerprint generated on your machine. A single key won't validate on a second device or browser. Each machine requires its own separately subscribed license at the standard price. There are no multi-seat discounts. See EULA §3 and §5 for the full policy.

How do I get app updates?

▾

Updates are available through the Download Portal at any time — no key needed to download. After replacing the file with the new version, re-enter your license key if Premium features aren't already unlocked. Updates are included at no extra charge for as long as your subscription stays active.

My machine was replaced — can I move my license?

▾

Yes — contact us with your order ID and a brief description of the situation (device replaced, browser reset, etc.) and we'll help you get re-activated on your new device. This is handled case by case rather than through an automated self-service tool, so response times can vary.

App & Technical

My app won't open or install.

▾

First, confirm your operating system meets the requirements listed on each app's detail page. On macOS, navigate to System Settings → Privacy & Security and allow the app if prompted. On Windows, click "More info → Run anyway" on the SmartScreen dialog. If the issue persists, contact us with your OS version and app version number.

Does the app require an internet connection?

▾

No — you can use every feature fully offline once the app is open. All document processing happens entirely on your local machine: there's no cloud processing and your documents never leave your device. The one exception is licensing: activating a key, and periodically re-confirming trial/subscription status when you do have a connection, involves a small network call carrying only a device ID and a timestamp — never your files or their content.
